our story

About Us

Innovation, reliability, safety, precision, customization, service and assistance. These are the values of AxA: a company founded in 1985, specialized in the design and production of counterbalanced cranes and hydraulic presses. At AxA, Research and Development always start from the customer’s needs. Our designers are called upon to find solutions, backed by decades of know-how. After a careful examination of the prototypes and pre-series, our cranes and our hydraulic presses go into production. Only after rigorous testing will they be able to merit the AxA brand and reach the industries for which they are intended.

The long history of AxA is a confirmation of the typical quality of Italian products that is renewed over time. Over the years AxA has been chosen as a partner of numerous renowned companies in Italy and around the world. The historical link with leading brands of mechanics has led us to serve a very demanding clientele. AxA headquarter is located in the province of Bologna, along the historic Via Emilia just a few minutes from Imola. We are in the heart of the Italian Motor Valley, a land that boasts a centuries-old mechanical tradition, expressed at the highest levels in theautomotive, medical, automation, mechatronics and packaging sectors. A land rich in technical schools of great value and many high quality laboratories. In this land our founders and our technicians were born and trained, absorbing day after day that blend of passion, technological culture and problem solving skills that have no equal in the world.

our mission

At AxA Group, we believe that every lift, every movement and every task should empower the people who perform it. This is why we devote our energy to creating lifting solutions and hydraulic presses that don’t just move materials—they elevate the quality of work itself. Driven by innovation and guided by care, we design sustainable, intuitive technologies that protect workers, enhance comfort and prevent injuries. Our mission is simple yet profound: to place people at the heart of every solution, transforming effort into ease and challenges into possibilities.

our vision

We believe that true innovation starts from the real needs of operators. That’s why every AxA product is designed to simplify daily tasks, enhance ergonomics and deliver consistent performance across workshops, production lines and field operations. At AxA Group, we don’t just build machines—we create tools that empower people and move industries forward.

AxA Group provides solutions for manufacturing, metalworking, automotive, aerospace, construction, energy, and general industrial applications. Our mobile workshop cranes and hydraulic presses are chosen by companies that need precision, ergonomics and flexibility.

Our cranes offer:

  • No front legs, ideal for narrow or obstructed areas
  • High manoeuvrability thanks to ergonomic design
  • Precise lifting control with double-action hydraulic pumps
  • Compact structure, perfect for indoor material handling
  • Made-in-Italy quality, ensuring durability and reliability

These features make AxA cranes a preferred choice for safe and efficient material handling.

AxA offers light-duty to heavy-duty mobile cranes, capable of lifting a wide range of loads depending on the model. Custom capacities can be engineered for specific applications.

We design manual, electric, and hydraulic workshop presses suitable for assembly, repair, testing and industrial production. Presses can be customised with:

  • Special tables and frames
  • Digital pressure control
  • Custom tools and jigs
  • Higher tonnage or reinforced structures

Our custom hydraulic presses help improve productivity and process precision.
